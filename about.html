Our Story

The Journey of RK Event Jhansi

Founder & Visionary

Meet Rahul Kumar

RK Event Jhansi was established with a singular vision: to bring world-class event management and premium photography to the heart of Bundelkhand. Founded by Rahul Kumar, our agency has grown from a passionate startup to the most trusted name in wedding planning in Jhansi.

With over a decade of experience, we understand that every celebration is deeply personal. Whether it's a grand royal wedding in Orchha or a cozy birthday party in Rajgarh, we pour our heart and soul into making it perfect.
